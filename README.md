# Open Contemplative Neuroscience

A curated list of open resources for studying meditation and contemplative practice with the
methods of neuroscience: datasets, analysis code, literature tools, key papers, concepts and the
people and organisations doing the work.

An entry is included if it is:

1. **open**: free to use, with code under an open licence and data downloadable or available on
   a documented request route, and
2. **useful to contemplative neuroscience**: either built for it, or a general tool that
   meditation studies actually rely on (EEG analysis, fMRI preprocessing, meta-analysis and so
   on).

General wellness apps, guided-meditation content and commercial products without open code or
data are out of scope.

Every entry is registered in plain YAML under [`knowledge/`](knowledge), and the lists below are
generated from it. The same data powers an [interactive explorer and paper finder](https://cation21.github.io/contemplative-neuroscience/).
Corrections and additions are very welcome: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Open data

### Meditation and contemplative practice datasets

- [BIDS formatted EEG meditation experiment data](https://zenodo.org/record/2536267) — 64-channel Biosemi EEG from expert and non-expert meditators, interrupted roughly every two minutes by probes rating concentration and mind wandering. Basis of Brandmeyer & Delorme (2018). About 9 GB, BIDS formatted. Also mirrored on OpenNeuro. *(EEG · open)*
- [EEGDash catalog (OpenNeuro + NEMAR, 700+ datasets)](https://huggingface.co/EEGDash) — Not a meditation dataset itself: a registry that loads any public BIDS EEG dataset into PyTorch/braindecode with one call. Useful for pretraining or pooling resting-state data as controls. *(EEG, MEG, iEEG, fNIRS, EMG · open)*
- [ENIGMA-Meditation consortium](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu/) — Not a public download: a consortium pooling meditation neuroimaging datasets for harmonised mega-analysis. Labs with data can join. *(fMRI, sMRI · on-request)*
- [L-FAME: Longitudinal Focused Attention Meditation EEG](https://huggingface.co/datasets/L-FAME-Dataset-Benchmark/L-FAME) — 64-channel EEG and questionnaires before and after six weeks of breath focus or one of two mantra practices, with benchmark tasks and code. Hosted on Hugging Face. *(EEG · open)*
- [NeuroStore (Neurosynth Compose)](https://compose.neurosynth.org/) — 30,000+ studies with pre-extracted activation coordinates. Filter for meditation studies to run your own coordinate-based meta-analysis in Neurosynth Compose. *(fMRI · open)*
- [OpenNeuro: meditation datasets](https://openneuro.org/search?query=meditation) — Search OpenNeuro for "meditation" or "mindfulness" to find BIDS datasets; every upload passes BIDS validation, and most are CC0. *(EEG, MEG, fMRI, sMRI · open)*
- PsiConnect: multimodal neuroimaging under psilocybin with meditation training — Multi-echo fMRI and EEG at rest and during guided meditation, music and movie watching, before and after psilocybin; half the cohort did eight weeks of meditation training. Described as an openly curated, reusable dataset. *(fMRI, EEG, sMRI, DWI · announced)*
- [ReSource Project (Max Planck)](https://www.resource-project.org/en/) — Nine-month, three-module mental training study (Presence, Affect, Perspective) run 2012–2016 in Leipzig and Berlin. Data are not openly posted; publications are listed by the Social Neuroscience Lab and data access is by collaboration. *(sMRI, fMRI, physiology, behavior · on-request)*

### Data archives

- [DANDI Archive](https://dandiarchive.org) — Mainly animal and intracranial data; relevant for mechanistic work. *(account)*
- [Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets) — Increasingly where EEG benchmarks are released. *(no key)*
- [NEMAR](https://nemar.org) — Run EEGLAB/MNE pipelines on OpenNeuro electrophysiology without downloading. *(no key)*
- [OpenNeuro](https://openneuro.org) — Every dataset passes BIDS validation; most are CC0. *(no key)*
- [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org) — Hosts several meditation EEG datasets, including the Delorme meditation set. *(no key)*

## Open code

### Electrophysiology (EEG and MEG) analysis

- [EEGLAB](https://sccn.ucsd.edu/eeglab) — Long-standing MATLAB toolbox (ICA, plugins, NEMAR integration), used by many meditation EEG studies. *(MATLAB)*
- [MNE-Python](https://mne.tools) — Preprocessing, ICA, time-frequency, source imaging and statistics for M/EEG. The default starting point. *(Python)*
- [specparam (formerly FOOOF)](https://specparam-tools.github.io) — Splits power spectra into aperiodic (1/f) and periodic peaks. Version 2.0 is still a release candidate and adds time-resolved and event-related models. Re-test any "alpha/theta increase" claim with it. *(Python)*

### Neuroimaging (MRI) analysis

- [fMRIPrep](https://fmriprep.org) — Robust, standardised fMRI preprocessing for BIDS data; the usual first step before harmonised analyses. *(Python)*
- [Nilearn](https://nilearn.github.io) — Connectivity, decoding and GLMs on fMRI in Python. *(Python)*

### Peripheral physiology

- [NeuroKit2](https://github.com/neuropsychology/NeuroKit) — Heart rate variability, respiration, EDA and ECG processing, for the physiology side of practice studies. *(Python)*

### Real-time, neurofeedback and hardware

- [BrainFlow](https://brainflow.org) — One API for many consumer and research biosensors (OpenBCI, Muse and others). *(Multi-language)*
- [EEG-Notebooks](https://github.com/NeuroTechX/EEG-ExPy) — Ready-made experiments for consumer EEG headsets, good for teaching and pilot data. *(Python)*
- [Lab Streaming Layer (LSL)](https://labstreaminglayer.readthedocs.io) — Time-synchronised streaming across devices; supported by most research EEG systems. *(C/C++, Python)*
- [MNE-LSL](https://mne.tools/mne-lsl) — Real-time streams and buffers integrated with MNE-Python, for neurofeedback-guided meditation. *(Python)*
- [OpenBCI](https://openbci.com) — Open hardware boards (Cyton, Ganglion) and GUI; a low-cost route to running your own meditation EEG study. *(Hardware + GUI)*

### Machine learning and EEG foundation models

- [braindecode](https://braindecode.org) — Deep learning for EEG in PyTorch, with model zoo and dataset loaders. *(Python)*
- [CBraMod](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod) — EEG foundation model with criss-cross attention; a strong recent baseline in benchmarks. *(Python)*
- [EEG-FM-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17742) — Benchmark for comparing EEG foundation models on standard tasks before choosing one. *(Python)*
- [EEGDash](https://github.com/eegdash/EEGDash) — Streams 700+ public BIDS datasets from OpenNeuro and NEMAR into PyTorch / braindecode. *(Python)*
- [LaBraM](https://github.com/935963004/LaBraM) — EEG foundation model; fine-tune on small meditation datasets instead of training from scratch. *(Python)*

### Meta-analysis

- [Neurosynth Compose](https://compose.neurosynth.org) — Curate studies, annotate them and run reproducible meta-analyses in the browser, backed by NeuroStore. *(Web)*
- [NiMARE](https://nimare.readthedocs.io) — Coordinate- and image-based meta-analysis (ALE, MKDA and more); the engine behind Neurosynth Compose. *(Python)*

### Text mining and paper embeddings

- [sentence-transformers](https://www.sbert.net) — Training and inference for contrastive text encoders; used in clm/ to fine-tune and embed the corpus. *(Python)*
- [SPECTER2](https://github.com/allenai/SPECTER2) — Scientific paper embeddings trained on citation triplets; a heavier but stronger alternative encoder. *(Python)*
- [Transformers.js](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js) — Runs ONNX models in the browser (WASM or WebGPU); powers the on-device paper finder. *(JavaScript)*

## Literature search and meta-analysis databases

- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org) — Metadata (including abstracts) is CC0, so it is safe to redistribute in the corpus. *(no key)*
- [bioRxiv / medRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org) — Where many neuroimaging meditation papers appear first. *(no key)*
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org) — No key needed. resultType=core returns abstracts, MeSH terms and licence info. Used by the finder's live search and the corpus builder. *(no key)*
- [NeuroStore / Neurosynth](https://compose.neurosynth.org) — Basis for custom meta-analyses of meditation fMRI. *(account)*
- [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org) — Since 13 Feb 2026 the API needs a free key (about $1/day of free usage; keyless is a small demo quota). Abstracts come as an inverted index. Best for citation pairs. Bulk snapshot remains free on S3. *(free key)*
- [PubMed (NCBI E-utilities)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) — MeSH term "Meditation" is the cleanest filter. Optional key raises rate limits. *(no key)*
- [Semantic Scholar API](https://www.semanticscholar.org) — Recommendations endpoint suggests recent papers from positive/negative seed papers. Low anonymous rate limits; request a key for heavy use. *(no key)*

## Key concepts

- **Focused attention (FA)** — Holding attention on one object (often the breath), noticing when it drifts and bringing it back. The cycle of wandering, noticing and returning is itself a common study target. See [Lutz 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2008.01.005), [Hasenkamp 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2011.07.008), [Brefczynski-Lewis 2007](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0606552104).
- **Open monitoring (OM)** — Non-selective awareness of whatever arises, without fixing on an object. Vipassana and many "open presence" practices fall here. See [Lutz 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2008.01.005), [Slagter 2007](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0050138), [Cahn 2010](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10339-009-0352-1).
- **Compassion and loving-kindness** — Constructive practices that cultivate care for others' suffering or wishes for their well-being. Distinct in brain and behaviour from empathic distress. See [Lutz 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0001897), [Klimecki 2014](https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nst060), [Weng 2013](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797612469537).
- **Non-dual awareness** — Practices aiming at awareness without a felt split between observer and observed. Studied mostly in very experienced practitioners. See [Josipovic 2014](https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.12261), [Dahl 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.07.001).
- **Mind wandering** — Thought that drifts away from the current task. Meditation studies use it both as the thing being trained and as a control state. See [Christoff 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2016.113), [Brandmeyer 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00221-016-4811-5), [Mrazek 2013](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797612459659).
- **Default mode network (DMN)** — Medial prefrontal, posterior cingulate and parietal regions active during rest and self-related thought. Reduced DMN activity is one of the most reported meditation findings. See [Raichle 2001](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.98.2.676), [Brewer 2011](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1112029108), [Garrison 2015](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13415-015-0358-3).
- **Neurophenomenology** — Pairing careful first-person reports with neural data so experience constrains the analysis. Proposed by Varela in 1996; now central to advanced-meditation research. See [Lutz 2015](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0039585), [Chowdhury 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2023.108694), [Yang 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhad408).
- **Advanced meditation (jhana, cessation)** — States and stages reported by highly experienced practitioners, such as the absorptions (jhanas) or cessation events where experience briefly stops. Usually studied with intensive sampling of one or a few adepts. See [Chowdhury 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2023.108694), [van 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10548-024-01052-4), [Yang 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhad408), [Chowdhury 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2024.120973).
- **Predictive processing and active inference** — Frameworks in which the brain minimises prediction error. Meditation is modelled as changing the precision (confidence) given to predictions and sensations. See [Laukkonen 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2021.06.021), [Sandved-Smith 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab018).
- **Aperiodic (1/f) EEG activity** — The broadband, non-oscillatory part of the power spectrum. Changes in it can masquerade as band-power changes, so newer analyses separate the two. See [Donoghue 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-020-00744-x), [Lin 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpsc.2024.09.012).
- **State versus trait effects** — State effects occur during practice; trait effects persist outside it, e.g. at rest or in sleep. Long-term-practitioner studies try to separate them. See [Cahn 2006](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.2.180), [Ferrarelli 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073417).
- **Active control groups** — Comparison programmes matched for expectation and effort but without meditation (e.g. the Health Enhancement Program). Needed to attribute effects to meditation itself. See [Kral 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abk3316), [Van 2018](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691617709589).
- **Attention residue** — Sophie Leroy's term for the part of attention that stays on a previous task after switching to a new one, lowering performance on the new task; it is larger when the first task is unfinished or will be resumed under time pressure. Cognitive psychology studies the same carryover as task-set inertia. Brief mindfulness practice may clear it, though evidence is still behavioural and there is little neuroimaging work yet. See [Leroy 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2009.04.002), [Leroy 2018](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2017.1184), [Kuo 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2015.04.023).

## About this repository

### Privacy

The paper finder downloads a small sentence encoder once (23 MB, int8, fine-tuned for this
field) and runs it locally with [Transformers.js](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js). What you paste is
never sent anywhere. The optional *Search beyond this collection* button is off by default; when
used, it sends only extracted keywords to Europe PMC (or OpenAlex, if you add your own key) and
re-ranks the results on your device.

### Run locally

```bash
python -m http.server -d site 8000     # then open http://localhost:8000
```

Edit the YAML in `knowledge/`, then regenerate the site data and the lists above:

```bash
pip install pyyaml
python scripts/build_kb.py
```
